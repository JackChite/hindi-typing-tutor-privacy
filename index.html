1. Introduction

This policy applies to Hindi Typing Tutor, an Android educational typing-practice app developed by Star Cheetah Studio. It explains local practice storage, physical keyboard input, Android backup and Google Mobile Ads.

2. Local Information

The app stores practice statistics and preferences locally in a Room database and app-private settings storage. This can include lesson progress, accuracy, WPM, CPM, correct and incorrect counts, session duration, stars, attempt dates, keyboard layout, font size, difficulty, sound, dark-mode and keyboard-display preferences.

3. What the Developer Does Not Receive

The app has no account or login system and does not provide a developer-operated cloud sync. The app itself does not upload typing transcripts or practice statistics to Star Cheetah Studio. Android system backup and third-party services are described separately below.

4. Network and Offline Use

The app requests INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE so the Home-screen AdMob banner can load and network availability can be checked. Local lessons and practice remain usable when an ad cannot load or the device is offline.

5. Google AdMob

The Home screen displays one anchored adaptive banner using Google Mobile Ads SDK 23.6.0. The app does not add interstitial, rewarded, native or app-open ads. Google’s SDK may automatically collect and share information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, including IP address (which may estimate approximate/general location), app interactions such as launches, taps and ad views, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as Android Advertising ID and app set ID. Google states that SDK data is encrypted in transit.

The app does not request precise location and does not add its own analytics or advertising database. It does not currently include a Google UMP consent form or an in-app personalized-ad control; applicable choices may depend on Google’s systems, regional requirements and Android/Google settings. See Google Privacy Policy.

6. Keyboard Input

Mangal Unicode and Kruti Dev 010 typing, including USB OTG keyboard events and virtual-keyboard input, are handled locally for practice. The app does not upload those input events or transcripts to the developer.

7. Permissions and Third Parties

The app declares Internet, network-state and Google Advertising ID permissions required by the ads SDK. USB host support is optional. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise location or storage/media permissions. The app uses Android/AndroidX, Kotlin libraries and Google Mobile Ads SDK. It does not add Firebase analytics, crash reporting or a separate analytics SDK.

8. Android Backup

Android backup is enabled. Depending on device and backup settings, the operating system may back up or transfer local practice records and preferences and restore them later. This system-managed backup is separate from the app uploading data to Star Cheetah Studio.

9. Retention and Deletion

Local data remains until reset, cleared or removed according to Android behavior. In-app Reset Progress removes practice results and progress while retaining preferences. Android Settings → Apps → Hindi Typing Tutor → Storage → Clear Storage removes local records and preferences. Uninstalling removes local app data; backup copies may remain under Android control.

10. Children’s Privacy

This educational app has no account or profile system for submitting personal information to the developer and has no age-verification feature. Parents or guardians can supervise use and manage device, backup and deletion settings.

11. Security

Local records are protected by Android app-private storage and device security. No storage method can be guaranteed absolutely secure.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated when app functionality or privacy practices change. The last-updated date will be revised when changes are made.
